Earthquake Damage Prediction (Nepal)

Predicting post-earthquake building damage using advanced ensemble ML techniques and nested cross-validation.

Home Projects Contact

Overview

This project focuses on developing a predictive model to estimate building damage levels following the 2015 Nepal earthquake. It combines Random Forest and LightGBM models in an ensemble approach, supported by rigorous nested cross-validation for reliable performance estimation.

Methodology

  • Task: Supervised classification of post-earthquake building damage.
  • Models: Random Forest (Randomized Search) and LightGBM (Optuna optimization).
  • Evaluation: Nested Cross-Validation (5×3)
    • 5 outer folds for unbiased performance assessment.
    • 3 inner folds for hyperparameter tuning.
  • Final Model: Ensemble of Random Forest and LightGBM for higher stability and accuracy.
  • Metric: Mean F1-Micro and Accuracy across outer folds.

Conclusions

The ensemble approach combining Random Forest and LightGBM achieved the most balanced and reliable results. These findings underline the importance of model diversity and systematic validation in large-scale predictive modeling.

This work demonstrates how data-driven intelligence can support disaster response, improving resource allocation and enabling faster post-earthquake interventions.
