Earthquake Damage Prediction (Nepal)

Predicting post-earthquake building damage using ensemble ML and nested cross-validation.

Home Projects Contact

Overview

This project develops a predictive model to classify the structural damage level of buildings after the 2015 Nepal earthquake. It combines Random Forest and LightGBM models in an ensemble approach, evaluated with rigorous nested cross-validation to ensure performance reliability and generalization.

Methodology

  • Task: Supervised classification of post-earthquake building damage.
  • Models: Random Forest (Randomized Search) and LightGBM (Optuna optimization).
  • Evaluation: Nested Cross-Validation (5×3)
    • 5 outer folds for unbiased testing.
    • 3 inner folds for hyperparameter tuning.
  • Final Model: Ensemble of RF + LightGBM for improved generalization.
  • Metrics: Mean F1-Micro and Accuracy across outer folds.

Conclusions

The ensemble of Random Forest and LightGBM achieved the most stable and accurate performance, demonstrating the strength of combining multiple models under a robust validation framework.

These results show how data-driven systems can support real-world disaster response, helping prioritize inspections and enhance resilience in post-earthquake recovery.
