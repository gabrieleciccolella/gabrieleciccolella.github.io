The idea

Understanding a classifier by building it from scratch.

A Python implementation of K-nearest neighbours that makes the classification steps explicit: feature normalisation, distance computation, neighbour selection and majority voting. The task is to distinguish benign and malignant samples in an academic dataset.

The approach

01

Implement

Build KNN decision logic directly, rather than delegating the classifier to a library.

02

Validate

Explore holdout, repeated random splits and stratified validation.

03

Measure

Inspect accuracy, error rate, sensitivity, specificity and geometric mean.

What I took from it

From implementation
to understanding.

Implementing the method directly helped me connect the mathematical definition of a model with its behaviour in code. This is an educational classification project, not a diagnostic product.
