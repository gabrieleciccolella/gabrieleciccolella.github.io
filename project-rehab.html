The idea

Comparing eight classifiers for human motion analysis.

My bachelor's thesis explored how supervised machine learning can distinguish rehabilitation exercises. I compared eight classifiers, focusing on consistent preprocessing and model evaluation.

The approach

01

Represent

Prepare motion features suitable for supervised classification.

02

Compare

Evaluate LDA, Decision Tree, Logistic Regression, SVM, KNN, Random Forest, Gaussian Naive Bayes and AdaBoost.

03

Interpret

Compare the reported accuracy of each model under the project's evaluation protocol.
