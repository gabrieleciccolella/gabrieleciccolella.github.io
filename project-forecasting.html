The idea

72 hours of history. A 24-hour forecast.

A deep learning project for day-ahead photovoltaic power prediction. A PyTorch encoder reads a 72-hour history, while an autoregressive decoder predicts the next 24 hours. Calendar, solar and weather features support the sequence model.

The approach

01

Engineer features

Build calendar and solar features, weather transformations, lagged observations and rolling statistics.

02

Train the sequence model

Use an encoder-decoder LSTM, MAE loss, scheduled teacher forcing, early stopping and a validation-based learning-rate scheduler.

03

Compare with a baseline

Report MAE, RMSE, WMAPE and MASE, and compare against yesterday's corresponding hourly PV power.

What I took from it

From implementation
to understanding.
